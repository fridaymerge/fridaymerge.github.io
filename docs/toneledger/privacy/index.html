ToneLedger Privacy Policy

Privacy for a local-first app.

This policy explains how ToneLedger handles information in the iPhone app.

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Overview

ToneLedger is a local-first guitar maintenance, string history, and string inventory app. No ToneLedger account is required for the first release. The app does not include ads or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information Stored On Your Device

ToneLedger stores the records you create on your device, including guitar details, string changes, setup snapshots, maintenance reminders, string inventory, notes, and any guitar or string package photos you add.

Guitar and string package photos are processed locally on device before being saved locally in the app.

Optional Network Requests

ToneLedger may send limited lookup requests to ToneLedger services when you use barcode lookup, string product search, or Price Watch. These requests may include a barcode, product search terms, or string product identity needed to find product metadata, retailer price results, or retailer search links.

These lookup requests are used to provide app functionality. They are not used for advertising or third-party tracking.

Permissions

  • Camera access is used for barcode scanning, guitar photos, and string package front photos.
  • Photo library access is used when you choose a guitar or string package photo from your library.
  • Notifications are used only for maintenance reminders you create.

Exports And Sharing

If you export or share records from ToneLedger, you choose where that exported file or text goes. FridayMerge does not receive your exported records unless you choose to send them to us.

Subscriptions

ToneLedger Unlimited subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. FridayMerge does not receive or store your payment card information.

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, email support@fridaymerge.com.
