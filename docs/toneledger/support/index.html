Common Topics

  • ToneLedger stores guitar records, string history, setup notes, reminders, inventory, and photos on your device.
  • No ToneLedger account is required for the first release.
  • Exports are created from the app and saved or shared only where you choose.
  • Subscriptions are managed through Apple. To manage or cancel, open iOS Settings, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.
  • Barcode scanning, guitar photos, and package photos require camera or photo library permission only when you use those features.
  • Price Watch checks supported retailer listings when available and keeps retailer search links separate from ranked prices.
